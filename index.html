The goal

Complete a row, column or diagonal (5 in a row).

How it works

  • Enter your name to get your own shuffled card.
  • When you spot something happen, tap the square.
  • Pick the session and note who said it. The time is stamped for you, in Munich time. That is your evidence.
  • It counts when it is said out loud or shown on screen in a session (the chat does not count). Marks count from 10:00 Munich time.
  • The centre square is free.

Winning

  • In the room: yell BINGO!
  • Remote: post BINGO with a screenshot of your card and activity log in #fabric-compete-roadshow-central on Slack. On a phone, scroll down for the log; on a laptop, zoom out (Ctrl or Cmd and minus) until both fit.
  • Claims are checked by hand from your screenshot. If a square is rejected, play continues.

Bonus: blackout

  • After the first line, keep going for a full card.
  • No self-triggering: saying it yourself to fill your own square does not count.
